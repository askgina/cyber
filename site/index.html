    Method

    Breach Ledger is rebuilt every hour from public feeds. Each run pulls the sources below, removes duplicates, recomputes the 24-hour, 7-day and 30-day counts, and republishes this page. Nothing here is a verdict: an entry records what a source claims, how far that claim has been corroborated, and where to read more.

    Confidence stages

    Claimed
    A single post, leak-site listing or unverified report. Treat as a lead.
    Corroborated
    Independent confirmation: a second credible source, on-chain transactions, or a statement from the affected party.
    Confirmed
    A post-mortem, regulator filing, CISA KEV listing or equivalent authoritative record.

    Ransomware leak-site listings are claims by criminal groups and are shown only as counts by group, sector and country. Victim names are deliberately omitted.

    Attack patterns are assigned by rules over each entry's text, DefiLlama's classification and CISA's CWE codes. Similarity combines text overlap (TF-IDF cosine) with shared patterns and shared vendors or chains; groups are entries whose similarity clears a fixed threshold. Crypto entries are also compared with DefiLlama's full archive of past hacks. Where an incident is significant, a written analysis with the attack chain, open questions and mitigations is added and may correct the automatic tags.

    Sources

    • CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, JSON feed.
    • ransomware.live leak-site index, v2 API.
    • DefiLlama hacks dataset, for crypto losses, chains and technique, polled live from your browser every two minutes.
    • SlowMist Hacked, merged with DefiLlama; each crypto entry shows which sources list it.
    • rekt post-mortems, linked to the matching exploit.
    • Have I Been Pwned breach catalog (CC BY 4.0), polled live every ten minutes.
    • News: The Record, The Hacker News, BleepingComputer, DataBreaches.net, Krebs on Security, rekt.
    • X: curated signals plus a live search link on every entry.

    Other trackers

    Useful alongside this page. Live threat maps show detections from one vendor's sensors rather than confirmed incidents, so they are linked here, not counted.

    • Crypto: rekt leaderboard, SCH Web3 hacks dashboard, SEAL 911 for active incidents.
    • Breaches and ransomware: ransomware.live, Axis Intelligence breach tracker, Cyber Pulse.
    • Threat maps: Kaspersky Cybermap, FortiGuard, SonicWall, and the DEFCON Level index.

    Limitations

    Feeds lag reality by minutes to days, and coverage is uneven: crypto and ransomware are well instrumented; most corporate breaches surface only through disclosure. Loss figures are as reported and often revised. News items are filtered by keyword and may include advisories rather than incidents.
